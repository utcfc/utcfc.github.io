This page contains an interactive comparison of theoretical predictions for the cosmic Population III (Pop III) star formation rate density (SFRD) evolution as a function of redshift, based on Figure 8 of the review from Venditti, Schaerer, et al. (2026). The archival data provided alongside this compilation include digitizations of published curves and data shared directly by their authors, and can be downloaded below.

NoteReproducibility

The compilation is intended for archival and visualization purposes only. Reconstructed data may not exactly reproduce the authors’ original underlying simulation outputs. For quantitative comparisons, consult the original model papers.

NoteCitation

If you use data from this compilation in a publication, please cite Venditti, Schaerer, et al. (2026) alongside references to the original works.

For corrections, or for adding further models to this page, refer to How to contribute.

Tentative observational constraints

Reference Description
Nagao et al. (2008) Subaru/Suprime-Cam photometric search for Lyα-He II λ1640 dual emitters with no metal line detections at 3.93 ≲ z ≲ 4.01 and 4.57 ≲ z ≲ 4.65. No convincing Pop III candidates with a Pop III SFR ≳ 2 M☉ yr-1 were found, yielding an upper limit of SFRD < 5 × 10-6 M☉ yr-1 cMpc-3 (assuming an IMF in the range [50, 500] M☉ from Schaerer (2003) and negligible escape fraction). The constraint in the range 4.0 ≲ z ≲ 4.6 is obtained considering the combined volume of the two redshift windows 4.03 × 105 Mpc3.
Cassata et al. (2013) 11 narrow He II λ1640 emitters identified from a sample of 277 galaxies with a highly reliable spectroscopic redshift at 2 < z < 4.6 from the VIMOS-VLT Deep Survey (VVDS) Deep and Ultra-Deep data. Interpreting their emission as powered by Pop III with a SFR of 0.1-10 M☉ yr-1 implies SFRD ≈ 10⁻⁶ M☉ yr⁻¹ cMpc⁻³ (assuming a top-heavy IMF and zero metallicity from Schaerer (2003)).
Fujimoto, Asada, et al. (2025) Revisiting JWST photometric Pop III candidate selection criterion from Fujimoto, Naidu, et al. (2025) following spectroscopic rejection of GLIMPSE-16043. Selecting strong Balmer jump, strong Hα and no [OIII] lines. Assuming AMORE6 is a genuine Pop III galaxy, the inferred UV luminosity function gives a tentative SFRD range (assuming a UV conversion factor for a moderately top-heavy IMF from Schaerer et al. (2025), with lower limit set by the detection of AMORE6 alone, and upper limit obtained by rescaling the general galaxy UVLF to match the upper boundary of the data point by AMORE6 and integrating it down to MUV = -12).
Mirza Khanlari et al. (2026) 8 He II λ1640-bright candidates with no UV metal-line detections at 1.9 < z < 2.3 identified in the Hobby–Eberly Telescope Dark Energy Experiment’s database. The estimated number density of ~30 Gpc-3 implies SFRD ≈ 6 × 10-9 M☉ yr-1 cMpc-3 , assuming 6 × 105 M☉ of Pop III stars per system per system according to Venditti, Graziani, et al. (2026) and a 3 Myr visibility time from Schaerer (2002).
